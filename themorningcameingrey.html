the morning came in grey

NEWArtwork — 2026

2026
Type

Shown at
Extraterestru — Piața de Artă și Design, Primitiv Plants greenhouse, Bucharest
Event

Materials
Exciter, Raspberry Pi, sound card, FIELD5 contact microphone (ATOMAT LAB), water, glass
Size
40 × 40 × 40 cm — mixed media

the morning came in grey is a generative listening post. It plays multi-layered ambients that slowly oscillate on their own, and turns the quakes and ripples it causes into sonic artifacts.

An exciter sends ripples and small earthquakes through a bowl of water. They work like an LFO on the whole ambience, and the resonances they leave behind are picked up by a FIELD5 contact microphone from ATOMAT LAB and folded back into the sound.

Shown in the Extraterestru exhibition by Piața de Artă și Design, in the greenhouse of Primitiv Plants in Bucharest, during PAD X at Mezanin. Visitors were welcome to interact with the microphones.
