Workshops

Hands-on sessions: microphones, sensors, circuits and collective composition.

Workshop — 2026

about:sound

Dâmbovița Delivery — Regie, Bucharest

Selected through an open call for Dâmbovița Delivery (3–4 October, Regie), about:sound was an open-air table where passers-by got hands-on with contact and electromagnetic microphones — listening to the hidden vibrations of objects and the electromagnetic hum of everyday devices, learning how the technology works and trying out basic ways to shape and manipulate the sounds they captured.

Workshop — 2026

Microphones, Code & Pen Plotter

Libra Bank Cultural Programme, Bucharest

A two-part workshop for Libra Bank employees, part of the bank's cultural programme. In the first sessions participants built their own contact and electromagnetic microphones, gathered sounds from around the bank, and composed an audio piece using only what they had recorded. In the second part they used p5.js and AI to develop a generative art algorithm that turned their composition into sound-reactive plots, which were then drawn on paper with a pen plotter.

Workshop — 2025

Sensors & Haptics for Performative Arts

NOD Makerspace, Bucharest

Participants learned how to build a glove-like wearable interface that uses sensors to modify sound in real time, translating gesture and motion directly into audio transformation.

Workshop — 2025

Start Pentru Tineri

subStejar, Târgu Lăpuș

Children from Târgu Lăpuș, Maramureș learned how to build two types of microphones, EMF and contact microphones. By collecting hidden and overlooked sounds from their surroundings, participants later composed a collective audio piece, transforming the subStejar location into a collaborative audio piece.

Workshop — 2025

Contact Mics & Preamps

CAV Gallery, Bucharest

Participants built DIY microphones together with preamps and explored recording techniques, later composing textures and rhythms in Ableton Live.

Workshop — 2024

Recycle Your Technology

Bucharest, Arad, Reșița, Brașov

A hands-on workshop where participants soldered and assembled electromagnetic microphones, then used them to capture electromagnetic rhythms in the gallery space. It later evolved into a series across Romania, where participants from different cities learned how to build microphones and explore local soundscapes.

Workshop — 2023

Contact Microphones & Composition

NOVA Festival, Bucharest

We explored the urban environment with microphones, creating a sonic map of the city and later transformed the sounds into a collaborative composition.
