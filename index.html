Contact Listening — ATOMAT LAB

FIELD5 — Phantom Powered Contact Microphone

Designed for adventurous sound explorers, FIELD5 is a rugged, waterproof contact microphone that reveals the hidden vibrations of materials and environments. Its phantom-powered circuit ensures clean, low-noise recordings, while the modular design allows you to attach accessories like spikes, magnets, or suction cups for any surface. Durable, precise, and built for the field, it’s a reliable tool for discovering sound in unexpected places.

For more details, please visit the Shop .
