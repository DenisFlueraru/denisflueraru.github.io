Biography Contact

I work where sound meets material — where ordinary streets remember themselves and small objects hold the weight of place. These pages gather installations, field recordings and tactile experiments that ask you to listen slowly and touch carefully.

Subtle motion, warm tones — an archive that prefers feeling over explanation.

Contact Mics and Preamps workshop at CAV Gallery

I ran a hands-on session inviting participants to build contact microphones and learn simple preamp circuits — tuning listening practice into making.

Publication / Object Book — Thessaloniki

Ruined Resonances — a journey through sound and form

Ruined Resonances synthesizes each location’s acoustic signature with an alginate imprint, inviting readers to hold and listen to place. With support from Culture Moves Europe, the project expanded its archive and practices across new sites.
