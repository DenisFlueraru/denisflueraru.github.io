Rewind
An interactive audio installation in collaboration with Alexandru Berceanu, part of the White Imprint Exhibition.

In a striking resolution in 2022, the Supreme Court decision in Roe vs. Wade was overruled, reopening the possibility for states to prosecute medical personnel for performing abortion procedures.

In Rewind, a bot reads fragments of the 1973 Supreme Court decision, gradually fading into the 2022 ruling. Two tape recorders loop the recordings, continuously transferring and degrading the message.
