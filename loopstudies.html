Denis Flueraru Loop Studies Menu

Loop Studies is an ongoing series of works exploring the search for the perfect loop. Each piece is based on a 30-second musical loop created through a blend of analog and digital tools, combined with field recordings.

The sound is then translated into a plotted drawing, where frequencies unfold into arcs and gestures. A surface imprint accompanies each plot, anchoring the loop in the physical world and connecting the sonic to the tactile.
