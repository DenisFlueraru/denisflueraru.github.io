Solara (2024)

During the Fusion Air Residency, I had the pleasure of collaborating with two scientists from the Institute for Space Science, Maximilian Teodorescu and Marius Echim. Our collaboration resulted in an interactive installation that explores the paradoxical relationship we have with the sun, entitled Solara.
