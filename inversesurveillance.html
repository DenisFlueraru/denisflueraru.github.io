Context & Concept
Inverse:Surveillance is an interactive installation that explores panopticism and the act of sousveillance. The work takes the form of a human-scale CCTV, allowing participants to look back at systems of power.

Interaction
The installation reflects on algorithmic authority, the loss of privacy, and how control systems are reinforced by our own actions. Visitors grab the CCTV antenna and peer through its eye to uncover what lies behind surveillance.
