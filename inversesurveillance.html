Denis Flueraru Inverse:Surveillance Menu

Context & Concept
Inverse:Surveillance is an interactive installation that explores Foucault’s idea of panopticism and the act of sousveillance. The work takes the form of a human-scale CCTV system, allowing participants to observe what usually remains hidden behind structures of power.

Interaction
The installation reflects on algorithmic control, the erosion of privacy, and how systems of power are often reinforced by our own behavior. Viewers are invited to grab the CCTV antenna and look through its eye, uncovering the mechanisms behind surveillance and authority.
