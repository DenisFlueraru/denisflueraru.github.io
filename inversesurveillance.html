Denis Flueraru Inverse:Surveillance Menu

Concept
Interactive installation exploring panopticism and sousveillance. The work takes the form of a human-scale CCTV device, allowing viewers to question who truly holds power and who is being observed.

Interaction
Visitors are invited to grab the CCTV antenna and peer through its eye. The piece reflects on algorithmic authority, loss of privacy, and the constraints we build ourselves — pushing toward a quietly dystopian future.
