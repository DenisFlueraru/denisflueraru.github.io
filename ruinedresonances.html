Ruined Resonances

Artwork — 2024
Year
2024
Type
Object book series — field recordings and casts
First edition
Thessaloniki
Support
Culture Moves Europe

A journey through sound and form. With the support of the Culture Moves Europe programme, Ruined Resonances broke boundaries for the first time, expanding our cross-modal archive with new locations.

This object book series explores the memory of places through both sound and form. Each edition combines field recordings with gypsum molds taken from streets and landmarks. The book synthesizes each location’s acoustic signature with an alginate imprint, attempting to teleport readers into spaces layered with history, and highlight the contrast between past and present.

As readers progress, they encounter casts that can be touched and recordings that can be listened to — deepening the connection to the site. The first edition was realized in Thessaloniki.
