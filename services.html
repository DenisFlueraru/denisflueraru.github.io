Services

01

Sound Design & Composition

Immersive soundscapes, field recordings and generative audio for installations, performances and bespoke compositions. Audio is crafted with analog media such as tape and organic recordings, and with digital tools, integrating MIDI and live interaction systems driven by human and non-human controllers — buttons, sensors and other interfaces. Voiceovers and post-production for podcasts, films, jingles, commercials and music, alongside live performances, workshops and audio repair. Further explorations use contact microphones, electromagnetic sensors and custom instruments.

  • Ableton Live
  • Pro Tools
  • Max MSP
  • Field recording
  • Voiceover
  • Post-production
  • Custom instruments
02

Interactive & Software Development

Dynamic interactive experiences built through software development, sensor integration and generative algorithms, with machine-learning interfaces for human recognition, gesture and environmental interaction. Projects span responsive installations, interactive performances and experimental systems, with a focus on real-time audio-visual feedback and immersive engagement.

  • C
  • JavaScript
  • Processing
  • Arduino
  • Max MSP
  • Machine learning
  • Sensors
03

3D Design & Fabrication

Models are created digitally for visualisation, planning and production. Physical prototypes and installations are realised through 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC machining and hands-on craftsmanship, then painted and finished so the conceptual, technical and aesthetic sides come together.

  • Rhinoceros 3D
  • Blender
  • 3D printing
  • Laser cutting
  • CNC
  • Finishing
04

Creative & Project Development

Ideas are turned into tangible projects through sketching, research and iterative brainstorming, aligned with the client’s goals or the project’s objectives. Structured planning balances conceptual exploration with practical execution, and collaboration keeps projects moving from initial vision to realised outcome.

  • Concept
  • Research
  • Planning
  • EU-funded project coordination
  • Teamwork
