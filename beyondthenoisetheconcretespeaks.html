Beyond the noise, the concrete speaks

Artwork — 2024
Year
2024 — 2025
Type
Casts and sound composition
Editions
Bucharest (2024), Brașov (2025)

The series explores the urban milieu and turns the streetscape into a tactile journey through memory and place. The molds collectively resonate with the vibrant interplay between the city's history and contemporary experience, woven into a sound composition crafted from field recordings taken on location.
