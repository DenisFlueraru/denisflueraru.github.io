Collab — 2025

Hush Glitches

National Dance College, Bucharest

Developed within a larger multidisciplinary team in collaboration with the Bucharest Institute of Biology, this performance explored the relationship between body, environment, and digital media. I designed a sensor-rigged shoe integrating humidity, temperature, pressure, and light sensors, enabling dancers to influence sound and video content in real time.

Collab — 2025

Artists Play with Time

Performances across the EU

A series of bespoke contact microphones and preamp boxes designed to amplify the subtle ticking of clocks as instruments for performers. From 3D modeling and printing to soldering, shielding, and sound testing — every detail was tuned to bring time into focus.
